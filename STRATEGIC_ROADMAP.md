# RepLog Strategic Roadmap

## Product Vision

RepLog should become **the Obsidian of strength training**:

- local-first
- account-free
- cloud-optional
- exportable
- durable
- deeply analytical
- intelligent without requiring subscriptions or remote APIs

The goal is not only to match Strong or Hevy. The goal is to become a private training operating system for serious lifters.

## Core Product Principles

1. **Own your training history** — local database, CSV/JSON export, import/restore.
2. **No forced cloud** — offline forever by default.
3. **No locked local basics** — workout logging, analytics, backup and templates stay accessible.
4. **Structured data first** — every future insight depends on clean exercise, set, muscle and movement metadata.
5. **AI later, rules now** — build the deterministic analytics engine before adding on-device models.

## V2: Better Than Strong

Focus: best Android workout logger.

- Smart exercise library metadata
- Exercise media support
- Advanced set types
- Supersets
- Streaks and milestones
- Advanced plate calculator with custom plates

## V3: Better Than Hevy

Focus: best local analytics platform.

- Lifetime analytics
- Muscle balance analysis
- Recovery score
- Plateau detection
- PR prediction engine

## V4: Killer Feature

Focus: Training DNA™ and adaptive programming.

- Volume tolerance
- Recovery rate
- Frequency response
- Rep preference
- Exercise preference
- Local rules engine
- On-device AI later via TensorFlow Lite or similar

## V5: Moat

Focus: training history as a motivational knowledge graph.

- Workout replay
- Progress timeline
- Strength forecasting
- Personal training knowledge base

## V6: Category Dominator

Focus: features difficult to copy.

- Local AI trainer
- Auto program builder
- Voice logging
- Camera rep counting later
