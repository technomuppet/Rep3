# RepLog Privacy Policy Draft

RepLog stores workout, exercise, set, bodyweight, template, backup and settings data locally on your device.

RepLog does not operate its own server and does not sell personal data. RepLog does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases in this build.

If you choose to export CSV or JSON backups, those files are created locally and can be shared using Android's system share sheet at your discretion.

Android backup may back up local app data according to the user's device settings.
