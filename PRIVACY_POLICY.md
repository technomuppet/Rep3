Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains how RepLog handles your information.
RepLog is designed to be private by default.

1. Offline-first, on-device storage
RepLog stores your data locally on your device. This includes your profile (such
as your display name and any optional details you choose to add), your workouts,
history, templates, progress and settings. The app does not require an account.

2. No data collection by us
We do not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal data. RepLog does not
include analytics, advertising or tracking software, and it does not request
internet access for its core functionality. Because your data stays on your
device, we cannot see it.

3. Information you provide
Any information you enter, including your display name and optional date of birth,
height, weight, units, experience level, goal, training frequency and equipment,
is stored only on your device and used only to personalise and operate the app.

4. Legal acceptance records
When you accept the legal documents, the app stores a record on your device
containing the accepted document versions, the date and time of acceptance, the
app version, your display name and confirmation that acceptance was completed.
This record is kept locally so you can review your acceptance history in Settings.

5. Exports and backups
If you use the export or backup features, files are written to a location you
choose on your device or storage. Once you share or move those files, they are
under your control and responsibility.

6. Permissions
The app requests only the permissions needed for on-device features, such as
notifications and vibration for the rest timer and access to a folder you select
for exports. It does not request internet or location permissions for its core
features.

7. Children
RepLog is a general fitness application and is not directed at children. Please
ensure use is appropriate and supervised where required.

8. Your control
Because all data is local, you remain in control. You can export your data, and
you can delete your data from within the app or by removing the app from your
device.

9. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy. Where it changes, you may be asked to review and accept
the updated version before continuing. Your existing data will be preserved.

By accepting, you confirm that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
