1. Educational purpose only
RepLog is an educational workout planning and logging application. It helps you

tool, not a medical device or a medical service.

RepLog does not provide medical advice. Nothing in the app, including suggested

physiotherapy, nutritional or other professional advice.

3. No diagnosis or treatment
RepLog does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease, injury or health
condition. Any information presented is general in nature and may not be
appropriate for your individual circumstances.

4. Your responsibility to decide if exercise is appropriate
You are solely responsible for determining whether any exercise, program or
activity in or suggested by the app is suitable and safe for you. You should
consider your own health, fitness level, medical history and any conditions or
limitations you may have.

5. Consult a qualified professional
You should seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional before starting
any new exercise program, particularly if you are pregnant, have or suspect a
medical condition, are recovering from injury or surgery, are taking medication,
or have any concerns about your ability to exercise safely. Where appropriate,
also seek guidance from a suitably qualified coach or trainer.

6. Inherent risks of exercise
Physical exercise, including resistance training, carries inherent risks. These
include but are not limited to muscle strains, sprains, joint and tendon injury,
falls, dropped or mishandled equipment, dizziness, fainting, and in rare cases
serious cardiac events or other serious injury.

All exercise undertaken in connection with the app is performed voluntarily and
at your own risk. You are responsible for using correct technique, appropriate
loads, suitable and well-maintained equipment, and a safe training environment,
and for warming up and progressing sensibly.

disclaimer affects your statutory rights.

9. Stop and seek help if you feel unwell
Stop exercising immediately and seek medical advice if you experience chest pain
or pressure, dizziness, fainting, severe pain, difficulty breathing, an
irregular heartbeat, or any other symptoms that concern you. In an emergency,
contact your local emergency services immediately.
